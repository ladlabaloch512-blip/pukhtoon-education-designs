Before You Start

The speaking test has 3 parts and takes 11 to 14 minutes. Check your equipment first, then answer each question out loud.

Headphones: wear them so you can hear each question clearly.
Microphone: keep it close and speak at a natural pace.
Quiet room: background noise lowers your recording quality.

Part 1: Introduction and Interview

In Part 1 the examiner asks about familiar topics. In Part 2 you talk for 2 minutes on a cue card. In Part 3 you discuss the topic in more depth.
