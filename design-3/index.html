What Your Band Score Means

IELTS scores are reported on a 9-band scale, from Band 1 (non-user) to Band 9 (expert user). Each module, Listening, Reading, Writing and Speaking, gets its own band score, and your overall score is the average of the four. Most universities ask for Band 6.0 to 7.5, so knowing what each band means helps you set a clear target.

9.0
Expert userFull command of English. Accurate, fluent and completely understood in every situation.
8.0
Very good userHandles complex language very well, with only occasional small mistakes.
7.0
Good userGood command of English and can handle detailed arguments, with some inaccuracies.
6.0
Competent userGenerally effective command of English despite some mistakes and misunderstandings.
5.0
Modest userPartial command of English. Can handle basic communication in familiar situations.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Pukhtoon Education free to join?
Yes. Creating an account is free, and free courses and free mock tests are open to every student. Paid courses and paid mock tests unlock when you buy a package.
How do I pay for a package?
Payment is manual and simple. Choose a package on the Packages page, then confirm your payment on WhatsApp at 0314-1513815. The admin activates your access after confirmation.
How is my band score calculated?
Listening and Reading answers are checked instantly against the official style conversion tables. Writing and Speaking are reviewed by the teacher, and every result is saved in your Results and History page.
Can I retake a test?
Yes. You can retake any test from the Mock Tests page, and every attempt is saved separately so you can compare your scores over time.
Which IELTS test should I take, Academic or General Training?
Choose Academic if you plan to study at a university, and General Training if you plan to migrate or work abroad. If you are not sure, ask the teacher on WhatsApp before you book your test.
