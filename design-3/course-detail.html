About This Course

This course is made for complete beginners who want to speak Pashto with confidence. You start with the alphabet and the sounds of the language, then move step by step into greetings, daily vocabulary and full conversations. No previous knowledge is needed.

Every lesson is a short video with clear examples and speaking practice, so you can study on your phone whenever you have a few free minutes. By the end of the course you will be able to introduce yourself, ask simple questions and hold a basic conversation in Pashto.

What You Will Learn

The Pashto alphabet and how each sound is pronounced
Everyday greetings, introductions and polite phrases
Numbers, days, months and telling the time
Family, home and village vocabulary you will use daily
Building simple, correct sentences of your own
Listening and speaking practice in every single lesson
