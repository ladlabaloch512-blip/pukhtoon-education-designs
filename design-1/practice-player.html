The Town Library

The town's public library first opened in 1962 as a single reading room with fewer than two thousand books. Today it occupies two floors and serves students, families and researchers from across the district. Membership is free for people who live in the town. Visitors from other places may also join, but they pay a small fee.

The main reading room opens at eight in the morning and closes at nine in the evening. The children's area opens one hour later, at nine, so staff can prepare activities before families arrive. The library lends books, magazines and films, but it does not sell them. Anyone who needs a quiet place to study can book a desk beside the large windows.

Evening classes are held on Tuesday and Thursday. They include computer skills, English conversation and help with school homework. On Saturday mornings, volunteers repair damaged books and cover new arrivals before they are placed on the shelves.

Demand for computers has grown quickly in recent years. Last year, the library added twenty new computers and created a small training room. Staff say the next goal is to extend the opening hours on Sundays, although no final decision has been made.
