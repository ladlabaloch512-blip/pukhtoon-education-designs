Rules of the Full Mock

  • There are no breaks between Listening, Reading and Writing. The next module starts as soon as you submit the previous one, exactly like the real test.
  • Every recording in Listening plays exactly once. There is no pause and no replay.
  • The timer never stops. When time runs out, the module is submitted automatically with the answers you have saved.
  • Speaking is recorded on your device microphone and saved to your attempt for review.
  • At the end you get one overall band score with a breakdown for all four modules, saved in your Results and History.
