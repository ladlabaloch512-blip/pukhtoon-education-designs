Package Questions

How enrollment, payment and access work.

How do I pay for a package?+
Tap Enroll via WhatsApp on the package you want and message us. We confirm your payment on WhatsApp (0314-1513815) and the admin activates your package on your student account. There is no online checkout and no card details are ever asked for on the website.
When does my access start and end?+
Your access period starts on the day the admin activates your package: 30 days for Basic, 60 days for Premium and 90 days for Ultimate. Your dashboard always shows your test countdown, and we recommend choosing a package that covers the weeks up to your official test date.
What happens when my access ends?+
Your account, results history and progress records stay saved. Paid courses and mock tests simply lock again until you renew or upgrade. Message us on WhatsApp to reactivate at any time.
Can I upgrade from Basic to Premium later?+
Yes. Many students start with Basic and upgrade when they get closer to their test date and want live sessions and essay grading. Contact us on WhatsApp and we will guide you through the upgrade.
Is anything free before I buy?+
Yes. A free account includes free video courses, a free Listening mock test and a free Writing mock test, so you can experience the full platform and see your starting band score before spending anything.
