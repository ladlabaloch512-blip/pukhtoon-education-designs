IELTS Modules

IELTS comes in two versions: Academic and General Training. Listening and Speaking are the same in both, while Reading and Writing change to match your goal. Choose your track below, open any module to see exactly what it covers, and then practise it in a timed mock test.

IELTS Academic

Academic Modules

For students applying to universities, colleges and professional registration bodies. Reading uses academic articles and journals, and Writing Task 1 asks you to describe charts, graphs and processes.

Listening

Understand lectures, talks and everyday conversations.

  • 4 sections and 40 questions
  • 30 minutes, played only once
  • Maps, forms and note completion
Practice Listening

Reading

Read academic articles, journals and research texts.

  • 3 long passages, 40 questions
  • 60 minutes, no transfer time
  • Headings and True False Not Given
Practice Reading

Writing

Describe data clearly and argue a position formally.

  • Task 1: charts and processes
  • Task 2: formal academic essay
  • 60 minutes for both tasks
Practice Writing

Speaking

The speaking test is shared by both IELTS tracks.

  • Part 1: familiar topic questions
  • Part 2: two minute cue card talk
  • Part 3: abstract discussion
Practice Speaking
IELTS General Training

General Training Modules

For candidates who need IELTS for work experience, training programs and immigration. Reading uses everyday texts from notices, adverts and handbooks, and Writing Task 1 is a letter.

Listening

Understand everyday speech, work and community talk.

  • 4 sections and 40 questions
  • 30 minutes, played only once
  • Conversations and announcements
Practice Listening

Reading

Read notices, adverts, manuals and magazine texts.

  • 3 sections and 40 questions
  • Everyday and workplace texts
  • 60 minutes, general interest topics
Practice Reading

Writing

Write letters and everyday essays in the right tone.

  • Task 1: formal or personal letter
  • Task 2: general interest essay
  • 60 minutes for both tasks
Practice Writing

Speaking

The speaking test is shared by both IELTS tracks.

  • Part 1: familiar topic questions
  • Part 2: two minute cue card talk
  • Part 3: abstract discussion
Practice Speaking

Academic or General Training?

Both versions test the same four skills at the same level. Only the Reading and Writing content changes, so pick the version that matches your goal.

PurposeStudy and professional registrationWork, training and immigration

Writing Task 1Describe a chart, graph or processWrite a formal or personal letter
