What Your Band Score Means

IELTS scores run from Band 1 to Band 9. Most universities and immigration programs ask for Band 6.5 to 7.5. Here is what each level says about your English.

9.0Expert User
Full command of English. Accurate, fluent and completely appropriate in every situation.
8.0Very Good User
Handles complex and detailed language well, with only occasional small inaccuracies.
7.0Good User
Operational command of English. This is the target most universities and employers ask for.
6.0Competent User
Generally effective command, especially in familiar situations, despite some errors.
5.0Modest User
Partial command. Can handle basic communication but makes frequent mistakes.
