Our IELTS Modules

IELTS has four modules, and each one is tested in its own way. Pukhtoon Education gives you lessons, practice sets and full mock tests for every module, so you always know exactly what to expect on test day.

Understanding Your Band Score

IELTS scores run from Band 1 to Band 9. Each module gets its own band, and your overall band is the average of the four, rounded to the nearest half band. Most universities and immigration programs ask for Band 6.5 to 7.5.

9.0

Expert User

Full command of the language. Accurate, fluent and completely appropriate in any situation.

8.0

Very Good User

Only occasional small errors. Handles complex and detailed language well.

7.0

Good User

Good control of the language with some mistakes. This is the level most top universities ask for.

TARGET 7.5
6.0

Competent User

Can use the language effectively in familiar situations, even with some errors and misunderstandings.

5.0

Modest User

Partial command of the language. Copes with the general meaning but makes frequent mistakes.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I start learning?
Create a free account from the login page, set your target band and test date in the onboarding steps, then take the free mock test. Your dashboard shows your current level and what to practise next.
Are the mock tests timed like the real IELTS?
Yes. Listening runs for 40 minutes with 40 questions, Reading runs for 60 minutes with 3 passages and 40 questions, and the full mock test combines all four modules in one sitting of about 2 hours 45 minutes, just like test day.
How is my Writing checked?
A teacher checks your Writing tasks in the admin panel. You receive a band score for Task Achievement, Coherence and Cohesion, Lexical Resource and Grammar, plus written feedback inside your results.
Should I choose Academic or General Training?
Choose Academic if you are applying for university study. Choose General Training for immigration and work visas. Both versions are available on the platform, and Listening and Speaking are the same for both.
