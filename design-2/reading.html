Reading Tips

01

Skim first, then scan

Spend two minutes skimming each passage for the main idea of every paragraph before you touch the questions.

02

Questions follow the passage order

For most question types the answers appear in the same order as the text. Work through the passage from top to bottom.

03

Watch the clock

Give each passage about 20 minutes. If a question is taking too long, mark it, move on, and come back at the end.
