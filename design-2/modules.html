IELTS MODULES

IELTS comes in two versions: Academic and General Training. Listening and Speaking are the same in both. Reading and Writing change with your goal, so pick the version that matches your plan and practise the right tasks from day one.

IELTS Academic

For students applying to universities, colleges and professional registration. The texts and tasks are academic, and Writing Task 1 asks you to describe visual data.

Listening

Conversations and lectures played once only, with questions on detail and opinion.

  • 40 questions in 40 minutes
  • Academic lecture in Section 4
  • Instant band score on submit
Start Practice →

Reading

Three academic passages taken from journals, books and research articles.

  • 40 questions in 60 minutes
  • Matching headings and features
  • Yes / No / Not Given practice
Start Practice →

Writing

Describe visual data in Task 1 and argue a clear position in Task 2.

  • Task 1 charts, graphs and processes
  • Task 2 discursive essay writing
  • Teacher marking and feedback
Start Practice →

Speaking

A structured interview on familiar topics, a long turn and a discussion.

  • Part 1 personal questions
  • Part 2 cue card long turn
  • Part 3 abstract discussion
Start Practice →

IELTS General Training

For immigration, work experience and training programs. The reading texts come from everyday life, and Writing Task 1 is a letter written for a real situation.

Listening

The Listening test is identical for Academic and General Training students.

  • 40 questions in 40 minutes
  • Everyday and workplace conversations
  • Instant band score on submit
Start Practice →

Reading

Everyday texts: notices, adverts, workplace documents and one longer passage.

  • 40 questions in 60 minutes
  • Section 1 social survival texts
  • Section 3 one long general text
Start Practice →

Writing

Write a formal or personal letter in Task 1 and an essay in Task 2.

  • Task 1 letter for a real situation
  • Task 2 general interest essay
  • Teacher marking and feedback
Start Practice →

Speaking

The Speaking test is identical for Academic and General Training students.

  • Part 1 personal questions
  • Part 2 cue card long turn
  • Part 3 abstract discussion
Start Practice →

Academic vs General Training

The two versions share Listening and Speaking. The table below shows exactly where they differ, so you can book the right test with confidence.

Part of the TestAcademicGeneral Training
Who It Is ForUniversity and college admission, professional registrationImmigration, work experience and training programs
ListeningSame test for both versionsSame test for both versions
Reading TextsAcademic articles, journals and researchNotices, adverts, workplace and everyday texts
Writing Task 1Describe a chart, graph, table or processWrite a formal, semi-formal or personal letter
Writing Task 2Essay on an academic topicEssay on a general interest topic
SpeakingSame test for both versionsSame test for both versions
ScoringBand 1 to 9 for every moduleBand 1 to 9 for every module
