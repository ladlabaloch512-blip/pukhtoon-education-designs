How Mock Tests Work

01

Pick Your Module

Choose Listening, Reading, Writing or Speaking, or take the full mock test. Free tests are open to every account, and paid tests unlock with any package.

02

Sit the Test Under Real Timing

The clock starts when you start, and the test submits itself when time runs out. Your answers save as you go, so a slow connection never costs you a test.

03

Get Your Band Score and Review

Listening and Reading are marked instantly with a question by question review. Writing and Speaking are checked by a teacher, and every attempt is saved in your results history.

Before you start: use headphones for Listening, keep a pen and paper ready for notes, and sit somewhere quiet for the full test time. Treat every mock test like the real exam and your real score will follow your practice score.
