Passage: The Evolution of Learning

Text size

Education has played a vital role in shaping communities throughout history. Historically, knowledge was passed down through generations orally, emphasizing practical life skills within the family and village. Elders taught children how to farm, build, cook and trade, and almost all of this teaching happened through speech, demonstration and daily practice rather than through books.

The advent of formal schooling changed how society prepares individuals, shifting focus toward structured learning. Classrooms, timetables and examinations replaced the flexible rhythm of learning at home, and qualifications began to matter for employment.

Today, modern education must adapt to rapid technological progress while maintaining core cultural values. In many traditional regions, blending informal, communal knowledge with formal schooling offers the best path forward: students gain recognised qualifications without losing the practical wisdom of their communities.
