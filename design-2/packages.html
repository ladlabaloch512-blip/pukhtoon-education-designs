Package Questions

How do I pay for a package?
Choose your plan, send the payment, and confirm it on WhatsApp at 0314-1513815. The admin verifies your payment and activates the package on your account. There is no card payment on the website.
How long does activation take?
Once your payment is confirmed on WhatsApp, the admin activates your access, normally on the same day. You will see the locked courses and mock tests open inside your account.

Yes. Message us on WhatsApp at 0314-1513815 and the admin will shift your account to the new plan and guide you about any difference in price.
Which package is best if my test is next month?
Premium is the best fit for most students with a near test date: all courses, all mock tests, weekly live classes and 24/7 mentor support for one full month of preparation.
