About This Course

UI/UX Design Fundamentals takes you from zero to a complete, presentable design project. You start with how users think and what research really means, then move into personas, journeys and information architecture before you draw a single screen.

From there you wireframe, build a working prototype in Figma, test it with real users and finish with a case study you can show to clients and employers. The first two lessons are free previews, and the full course is free to enrol for every Pukhtoon Education account.

What You Will Learn

  • How user research works and why it matters
  • Building personas and user journey maps
  • Wireframing screens step by step
  • Prototyping in Figma from a blank file
  • Running a simple usability test
  • Presenting a complete design case study
